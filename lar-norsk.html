The V2 rule

In a Norwegian statement the verb always sits in position 2. If you start with something other than the subject, the subject jumps behind the verb.

Hvaspiserdu?

Yes/no questions put the verb first: Spiser du fisk? “Do you eat fish?” Norwegian has no helper “do”.
